Image identity and date
The source book labels the upper photo on PDF page 58 as "10. Loess country." The narrative under Sunday, June 20 [1937], refers to photographs 10 and 11 during the party's northward journey through loess country toward Yenan (Yan'an). This is a 1937 travel landscape from the T. A. Bisson expedition, not a photograph verified as Edgar Snow's work, and not verified as Bao'an town. Do not label it as Snow, Bao'an, or as taken in 1936.
